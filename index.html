Ideation Engine — Workflow

What

One portal where a Creative Director connects a client's YouTube channel and knowledge base. The engine pulls a wide but curated data set, refines it against the client's persona and DFM's standards, and presents topics the CD iterates on live — a gamified loop that only ends when every standard is hit. Each locked topic ships with a research brief, sample packaging, and a client-calibrated predictive score, and the system self-scores against the YouTube Performance Standard's locked 7-day target.

How

The Connect step scopes the client-specific layers — knowledge base, channel, calibration. The Dragonfruit proprietary data lake (Turso, A/B database, standards) is shared and read-only during ideation: every connected session benchmarks against the same cross-client pool. Writes back into Turso are keyed by client, so ten CDs running ten clients simultaneously never collide — they're reading one shared brain and writing to their own client's rows.
